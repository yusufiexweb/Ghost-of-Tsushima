When the Mongols invade Tsushima, Jin Sakai sees his samurai forces overwhelmed and his home falling to a ruthless campaign. To save his people, he must question the traditions he has sworn to uphold.

03 / A world that speaks

No markers.
Just the wind.

Let the guiding wind lead toward a distant objective. Golden birds draw attention to hidden places; foxes lead to quiet Inari shrines. Smoke on the horizon may signal a village in need—or a Mongol outpost waiting to be challenged.

Across forests, fields, and coastal roads, Tsushima is more than a battlefield. It is a living landscape that invites you to look, listen, and find your own way.
